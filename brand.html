01 / IDENTITY

Quiet practice. Steady mastery.

RIFTMASTERY

Use the Jade Gate emblem and a single-line wordmark. Two angular gate pillars frame a luminous central rift: a symbol of focused practice and progression. Keep one-quarter of the mark’s width clear on every side. Minimum mark: 24px; preferred: 32–40px.

Voice

Direct, specific, and encouraging. Name the next action. Describe evidence without pretending it proves more than it does.

“Choose one question to test.”
“Save the result when you’re ready to review.”

Avoid hype, artificial urgency, guilt for missed days, and interruptions during play.

02 / COLOR

A small palette, clear roles.

Dark surfaces carry the interface. Ivory carries information. Muted jade marks practice and progress. Gold marks the primary action and selected state. Status colors always accompany words or symbols.

14 / body · 1.5 line height · 13 / supporting text

Literary headings. Clear controls.

Georgia for expressive headings, with a serif fallback. Inter when locally available, then the device’s native UI font for controls, notes, and data. Weight 400 for display type, 600 for functional labels. No external font request.

Spacing scale

4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64

Use 8–12px within a control group, 16–24px inside cards, and 32–48px between major sections.

Shape

3px controls · 2px panels · straight progress bars. Separate related sections with spacing and thin rules. Preserve circles only where the information or icon requires them.

04 / ICONOGRAPHY

Symbols of practice.

05 / COMPONENTS & STATES

Predictable at every touch.

Actions

One dominant action per task. A gold focus ring stays visible for keyboard users. Disabled controls remain clearly unavailable.

Interactive samples only; no app data is changed.

Persistent labels, readable hints, and at least 44px touch targets.

✓ Saved! Needs review× Couldn’t save

Empty states

Say what belongs here, explain why it helps, then offer one useful starting action. Leave charts out until there is evidence to display.

Motion & accessibility

160ms state transitions. No ambient pulsing or decorative loops. Respect reduced motion. Keep text contrast at 4.5:1 or higher; never rely on color alone.

06 / ART DIRECTION

The Cultivation Archive.

Each workspace has its own composition: a painted courtyard for practice, six connected realms for Progress, numbered legend folios for decks, parchment entries for the journal, and an artwork-led personal card archive. Shared ink, jade, gold rules, and typography hold the screens together. Images never replace accessible names or practical controls.

Etched mountain silhouettes, a distant temple, and lunar geometry create depth without competing with the practice. Antique-gold hairlines frame the artwork. Display headings use a tight editorial scale; actions remain clear and compact. Reserve the landscape for major entry points and progression. Keep the warrior’s face free of text and overlays.

A portrait within the hall

Home and Train reserve space for the sleeping warrior, within a shared header composition. Text and controls stay outside the artwork, and no dark overlay crosses his face.

Desktop: artwork meets the header edge, with a narrow fade on the left.
Phone: a full-width artwork band above the copy, fading only at its lower edge.
Crop: 65% horizontal / 28% vertical focal position.

If a new layout cannot keep his full face visible, change the layout. Keep this supplied artwork intact; use the Jade Gate emblem as the app identity.
